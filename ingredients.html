Ingredients

Cold-pressed Palm Christi and botanical herbs, rooted in a family-owned garden tradition.

At the heart of every bottle is Palm Christi — castor oil cold-pressed from seeds grown on our family land in southeastern Haiti. It is blended with arnica, calendula, clove, ginger, goldenseal, grapefruit, lemongrass, peppermint, and turmeric to support body comfort and everyday wellness rituals.

Every ingredient is chosen with intention — honoring Haitian healing traditions and the land that sustains us.
