The ritual

The Ritual

A practice of care, connection, and renewal — rooted in ancestral wisdom and therapeutic touch.

With mindful intention, apply the oil through massage and touch. Allow the healing botanicals to nourish your skin, calm your body, and restore your spirit.

A ritual of care, connection, and renewal.

❧
1

Prepare your space

Find a quiet moment. Warm the room if you can, breathe deeply, and set an intention for your body — rest, release, or renewal.

2

Warm the oil

Pour a small amount into your palms and rub them together. The warmth helps the botanicals absorb and deepens the sensory experience.

3

Massage with intention

Apply with slow, deliberate strokes — toward the heart on limbs, in gentle circles on the abdomen, and with care along the neck and shoulders.

4

Rest and receive

Allow the oil to absorb. Take a few breaths, notice how your body feels, and carry the calm with you into your day.
